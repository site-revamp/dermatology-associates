# Dermatology Associates — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Dermatology Associates is a private dermatology group established in Singapore in 2005. Its website lists four dermatologists and three clinics at Paragon, Novena and Connexion, providing medical and aesthetic dermatology for local and international patients.

The redesigned homepage improves readability, introduces direct care pathways and makes clinic information easier to compare before requesting an appointment.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. Larger, More Readable Typography

Increased text sizes, stronger headings and enlarged contact buttons replace existing styles as small as 11–13px. No text is below 15px. These changes make content and contact actions easier to read, particularly on mobile.

### 2. Direct Care Pathways

Six care pathways include descriptions and direct links, arranged in three columns on desktop and two on mobile. Previously, visitors relied on the wider navigation to find condition information. The new pathways provide clear starting points directly on the homepage.

### 3. Clearer Clinic Information

An early clinic selector presents branch-specific contact details, making addresses, phone numbers and hours easier to scan than the previous dense adjacent sections.

Paragon’s two Saturday closures each month are highlighted, while persistent mobile appointment actions keep contact options accessible.

## Design Improvements

- **Typography and hierarchy:** Larger text, stronger headings and enlarged buttons clarify content and actions.
- **Responsive layout:** Care pathways use three desktop columns and two mobile columns.
- **Consistency:** The selector groups clinic-specific details and highlights scheduling exceptions.
- **User experience:** Direct care links and persistent mobile appointment actions simplify discovery and contact.
