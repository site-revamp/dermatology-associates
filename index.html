Before your consultation

A little preparation.
A clearer conversation.

Prepare for your appointment and make time to discuss your concerns with your dermatologist.

Read the first-visit guide
  1. 01
    Arrange your appointment

    Contact your chosen clinic. If you hope to have a procedure during the visit, tell the team so they can advise on scheduling.

  2. 02
    Bring the relevant information

    Bring your referral letter, if applicable, and any medical insurance card or forms. Let the clinic know about drug allergies and relevant medical information.

  3. 03
    Discuss the options

    Your dermatologist will assess your concerns and discuss treatment options, suitability and possible complications.

Choose your clinic
How do I request an appointment?

Choose Paragon, Novena or Connexion above, then call that clinic or email appt@dermassoc.com.sg. Include your preferred clinic in the enquiry and wait for the team to confirm availability.

Where is the former Gleneagles clinic?

The official contact page identifies the Novena clinic as formerly Gleneagles. Its address is 10 Sinaran Drive, #11-01 Novena Medical Center, Singapore 307506. Call 6471 1733 for appointment enquiries.

Is Paragon open every Saturday?

No. The published schedule lists Saturday hours of 8.30am–12.30pm, with closures on two Saturdays each month. Please call 6333 8108 to confirm before visiting.

Are teleconsultations available?

The clinic contact page lists teleconsultation for selected conditions at Novena and Connexion. Contact the relevant clinic to ask about suitability and arrangements.

Does the practice see children?

Yes. The practice provides care for adult and paediatric skin conditions. Dr Chan Yuin Chew has a subspecialty interest in paediatric dermatology. Contact the clinic to discuss your appointment.
